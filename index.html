M3U8Player is a Chrome extension that implements an HTTP Live Streaming client. You can play .m3u8 HTTP Live Streaming (HLS). It relies on HTML5 video and MediaSource Extensions for playback. It works by converting MPEG-2 transport streams and AAC/MP3 streams into ISO BMFF (MP4) fragments. Web Workers are used for asynchronous transcoding if available in the browser. It also supports HLS + fmp4.

What is M3U8?

M3U8 is a UTF-8 encoded plain text playlist format. It does not store video or audio content itself, but records URLs of media resources for players to load and play corresponding media.

It is widely used: for online radio streaming, local music/video playlists. With M3U8 lists, you can play multiple media files with one click.

It supports absolute paths, relative paths, and online URLs. Originally designed for audio playlists, it is now widely used for online videos and live streaming, compatible with most major players.

How to Open M3U8 Files?

You can open and view M3U8 code with any text editor (Notepad, etc.), but text editors cannot play media.

To play M3U8 streams, use professional players like VLC, iTunes, XMMS (Linux), CocoModX (Mac).

You can also use online M3U8 players. Note: online tools only support URL-based M3U8, not local files, and require the stream to be accessible online.

FAQ

What is M3U8 Protocol?

HLS streaming index protocol, widely used for online video delivery.

How to Play M3U8 Videos?

Open the link with streaming-supported players, browsers or parsers.

Advantages of M3U8?

Segment loading, low buffering, mobile-friendly, adaptive bitrate.

M3U8 vs Regular Video Formats

M3U8 is a playlist, not a video file. MP4 etc. are complete video files.

Common Video Formats

MP4, FLV, MOV, AVI, MKV, WMV, WebM, etc.

M3U8 for Live Streaming

Real-time slicing, client fetches segments for low-latency live broadcast.

How M3U8 Works

Video split into small TS chunks; M3U8 records URLs and loads segments sequentially.

How to Parse M3U8

Read playlist, extract segment URLs, merge into complete video.
