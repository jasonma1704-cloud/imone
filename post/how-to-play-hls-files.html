How to Play HLS Files

HLS (HTTP Live Streaming) files are usually in M3U8 format. You can play them through multiple methods on different devices.

Play HLS in Browser

Modern browsers support HLS playback. Use our online player: paste the HLS URL and click play.

Play HLS with Desktop Player

Open VLC, select Media > Open Network Stream, then paste the HLS URL to start playback.

Play HLS on Mobile

On iOS, Safari supports HLS natively. On Android, use a browser or dedicated media app to play HLS streams.

Troubleshooting

If playback fails, check the link validity, network connection, and whether the stream is encrypted or expired.
