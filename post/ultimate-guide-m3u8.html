【Ultimate Guide】Everything You Need to Know About M3U8 Files

This ultimate guide covers everything about M3U8 files, from basic concepts to practical operations.

What Is M3U8?

M3U8 is a text-based playlist file used by HLS protocol, which records the location and order of video segments.

How Does M3U8 Work?

The client reads the M3U8 file, downloads each segment, and plays them sequentially to form continuous video playback.

How to Open and Play M3U8?

You can play M3U8 in browsers, desktop players, mobile apps, or convert it to MP4 for offline use.
