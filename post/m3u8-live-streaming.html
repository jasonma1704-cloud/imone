How Is M3U8 Used in Live Streaming?

M3U8 plays a key role in live streaming as the core of the HLS (HTTP Live Streaming) protocol:

Real-Time Segmentation

Live streams are cut into short TS segments in real time, typically about 10 seconds each.

Dynamic Playlist

The M3U8 file is updated continuously: new segment URLs are added and older entries are removed to keep the playlist length manageable.

Low-Latency Playback

The player keeps fetching the latest M3U8 and segments for a near-real-time live experience.

Adaptive Bitrate

Multiple bitrate variants let viewers choose a quality level that matches their network.

Wide Use Cases

Used for sports, news, online concerts, and many other live events.

M3U8 is designed to make live streaming both efficient and flexible for modern delivery needs.
