Advantages of MP4 Format

MP4 is the most popular video encapsulation format, widely used in local storage, video production and daily sharing.

Ultra High Compatibility

All devices, players and operating systems support MP4. It is the preferred format for video file sharing and transmission.

Balanced Quality and Volume

With advanced encoding technology, it keeps clear image quality while maintaining a relatively small file size, saving storage space.

Convenient Local Playback

As a complete single file, it can be played directly without parsing index files or loading segmented resources.

Rich Codec Support

It supports H.264, H.265 and other mainstream codecs, adapting to high-definition, ultra-high-definition and other different video production needs.
