How Is M3U8 Different from Regular Video Formats?

M3U8 differs from traditional formats (such as MP4 and AVI) in structure and purpose:

File Content

M3U8: A plain-text playlist listing segment URLs—it does not contain actual video data.

Regular formats: Binary files that store encoded video and audio directly.

Playback

M3U8: Streamed playback with on-demand segment loading and adaptive bitrate.

Regular formats: Often require a full download or local file before playback.

Use Cases

M3U8: Online streaming, live broadcast, and VOD services.

Regular formats: Local storage, offline playback, and file sharing.

Trade-offs

M3U8 excels at network delivery and user experience; traditional formats are often simpler for local editing and broad offline compatibility.

Understanding these differences helps you pick the right format for each scenario.
