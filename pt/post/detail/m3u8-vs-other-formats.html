M3U8 VS Other Video Formats

Compare M3U8 with mainstream video formats to understand their differences in streaming, compatibility and usage scenarios.

M3U8 vs MP4

MP4 is a complete single file, suitable for local storage. M3U8 is a stream index file, designed for network live streaming and on-demand playback, with better network adaptability.

M3U8 vs FLV

FLV relies on Flash technology which is now deprecated. M3U8 is based on native HTML5 and HLS protocol, supporting all modern browsers and mobile devices.

M3U8 vs WebM

WebM is a web-oriented encapsulation format. M3U8 focuses on stream segmentation and dynamic bitrate adjustment, and is more dominant in large-scale live broadcast scenarios.

Summary of Differences

Choose M3U8 for web live streaming and on-demand services. Choose complete video formats such as MP4, MKV for local playback and file storage.
