Common Video Formats

There are many video encapsulation formats on the market, each designed for different usage scenarios.

MP4

The most universal format, for local storage, sharing and general network on-demand.

MKV

Open multimedia container, supports multi-audio tracks and multi-subtitles, favored by movie enthusiasts.

AVI

Classic old format, large file size, gradually replaced by newer formats.

MOV

Apple QuickTime format, widely used in video creation and post-production.

M3U8

Streaming playlist format, dedicated to web live broadcast and video on-demand services.

WebM

Open source web format, optimized for HTML5 page playback.
