M3U8 Live Streaming Working Principle

Live streaming M3U8 is dynamically updated. Old segments are deleted and new segments are added continuously.

Stream Generation

Live video is captured and encoded on the server, then split into small TS files, and the M3U8 index list is refreshed in real time.

Client Pull Stream

The client regularly re-requests the M3U8 file, loads new segments sequentially, and realizes uninterrupted live playback.

Delay Feature

HLS live stream has a certain delay due to segmentation and buffering. It is suitable for general live broadcast rather than ultra-low delay interactive scenarios.
